Key Curriculum Insights

1. Escape-Heavy Curriculum

~34% of techniques are escapes, emphasizing defensive fundamentals for beginners

2. Guard Dominance

56% of sport BJJ focuses on guard positions (both playing guard and passing guard)

3. Striking Defense Priority

Over half of self-defense curriculum addresses punch/strike scenarios

4. Consistent Structure

Every week has exactly 6 numbered techniques (1 self-defense per class + 2 sport BJJ per class)

5. Cyclical Learning

4-week and 8-week cycles ensure position review throughout the 16-week program

6. Armbar Emphasis

The armbar is the most frequently taught submission (8 variations), reinforcing this fundamental attack

7. Cultural Integration

Each week includes a GB-specific cultural/philosophical topic to build community values
