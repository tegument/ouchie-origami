## Key Curriculum Insights

1. **Escape-Heavy Curriculum**: ~34% of techniques are escapes, emphasizing defensive fundamentals for beginners

2. **Guard Dominance**: 56% of sport BJJ focuses on guard positions (both playing guard and passing guard)

3. **Striking Defense Priority**: Over half of self-defense curriculum addresses punch/strike scenarios

4. **Consistent Structure**: Every week has exactly 6 numbered techniques (1 self-defense technique per class + 2 sport BJJ techniques per class)

5. **Cyclical Learning**: 4-week and 8-week cycles ensure position review throughout the 16-week program

6. **Cultural Integration**: Each week includes a GB-specific cultural/philosophical topic to build community values

7. **Armbar Emphasis**: The armbar is the most frequently taught submission (8 variations), reinforcing this fundamental attack

8. **Bottom Position Focus**: More techniques teach escaping/defending than attacking, appropriate for GB1 fundamentals

9. **Progressive Drilling**: Drills combine multiple techniques into sequences, building muscle memory for common transitions

10. **Balanced Self-Defense**: All major threat categories covered - strikes, headlocks, and clinch holds
